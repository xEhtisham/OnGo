About this event

Live Music Experience

Experience the biggest open-air music festival of the season under the autumn sky. Summer Sounds 2025 brings together leading indie rock, synth-pop, and soul powerhouses across two outdoor stages. Featuring 8 live acts, interactive light installations, curated artisan food pop-ups, and a vibrant community atmosphere designed for pure sonic immersion.

Set in the sprawling green amphitheater grounds of the Peshawar Sports Complex, this year’s production features stadium-grade acoustic engineering, 360-degree festival lighting, and immersive projection mapping by acclaimed regional visual artists. Whether you choose front-row pit access or open lawn lounging under the stars, Summer Sounds delivers an unforgettable night of high-octane performance.
